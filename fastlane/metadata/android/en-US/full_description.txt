HTFU is a no-nonsense workout tracker. It runs completely offline: it has no internet permission, no ads, no trackers and no account. Everything you log stays on your phone.

Programs
New to training? Tell HTFU your goals and it suggests plans to start with. Or follow any ready-made program or build your own. Built in:
- Bodyweight: 100 Pushups, 20 Pullups, 100 Squats, Recommended Routine, Bodyweight Routine, Murph, Workout of the Day
- Barbell: Stronglifts 5x5, Starting Strength, 5/3/1, nSuns, GZCLP, Greyskull LP, Phrak Greyskull, Texas Method, Madcow 5x5, Push Pull Legs, Upper Lower, Full Body 3x, YOLO
- Kettlebell: Simple & Sinister, Swing Ladder
- Running: Couch to 5K, Bridge to 10K, Half Marathon Novice 1, Beep Test
- Fitness tests: Army AFT, Marine PFT, Navy PRT, Air Force PFRA, CF EXPRES, IDF Combat Test, Bar-Or, police and firefighter tests (Ontario PREP, PIN and more)
- Mobility and cooldown routines
- Custom plans with your own exercises, sets, reps, weights, times and distances

Training
- Automatic progression: weights go up after a full session and deload after repeated misses
- Big tap targets for logging sets, a rest timer that keeps running in a notification, and optional voice cues
- Hands-free rep counter that uses the phone's proximity or motion sensor
- Plate calculator and % of 1RM calculator
- Exercise how-to guides with form cues
- Imperial (lb, ft/in) or metric units
- Six themes, including high-contrast colours

Progress
- Charts for every lift, personal bests, streaks and a calendar
- Body weight and measurements with optional progress photos (stored only on your phone)
- Achievements in sections (powerlifting, free weights, bodyweight, consistency, volume, running, programs), with a celebration when you earn one

Reminders and widget
- Workout-day reminders at the time you choose, re-armed after a reboot
- A home-screen widget that shows today's workout

Your data
- Back up everything (plans, logs, settings and progress photos) to one file and restore it on any phone
- Export your log as an Excel or CSV file
- Share a plan or a session with a friend as a file and compare on the leaderboard

Permissions: vibrate (rest timer and rep feedback), notifications (reminders and rest timer), run at startup (re-arm reminders after a reboot), and exact alarms (reminders and the rest timer go off on time). Nothing else.
